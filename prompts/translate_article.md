# Full Article Translation Prompt

## Task

Translate the cleaned English article you just produced into Traditional Chinese.

Translate the complete article faithfully and naturally. Preserve its meaning, tone, paragraph order, headings, quotations, lists, captions, and other meaningful structure.

Do not summarize, shorten, explain, critique, or add information. Keep names and technical terms accurate; when a term has no natural Chinese equivalent, retain the English term where helpful.

## Output

Return only the complete Traditional Chinese translation in Markdown.

Do not add commentary, translator notes, metadata blocks, or code fences.
